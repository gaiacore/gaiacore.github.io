What is Chakra Psychology?

Understanding the Mind, Healing the Heart, Awakening the Soul

At Gaia Core Sacred Space, Chakra Psychology is a unique therapeutic approach that integrates modern psychology with ancient spiritual wisdom. Rather than viewing emotional, mental, physical, and spiritual wellbeing as separate, Chakra Psychology recognises that they are deeply interconnected.

Each chakra represents a stage of human development, a psychological theme, and an aspect of consciousness. Life experiences, trauma, limiting beliefs, and emotional wounds can create energetic imprints that influence how we think, feel, relate to others, and experience life.

By exploring the psychology of each chakra, we begin to understand the deeper patterns that have shaped our lives. Through conscious awareness, compassionate enquiry, and energetic healing, these patterns can gradually transform — allowing greater freedom, authenticity, and inner peace.
